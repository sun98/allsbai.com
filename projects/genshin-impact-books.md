# Genshin Impact Books

[Open the reading room](https://genshinbooks.allsbai.com/) · [Browse all books](https://genshinbooks.allsbai.com/books) · [Quest notes & letters](https://genshinbooks.allsbai.com/books?kind=quest-text)

An independent fan reading room from [allsbAI.com](https://www.allsbai.com/), with English books and readable quest texts from Genshin Impact.

[![The Genshin Impact Books reading room, showing featured English book texts](../assets/library-preview.png)](https://genshinbooks.allsbai.com/)

- Browse by region or search for a title, character or passage.
- Move between volumes and related entries using the contents and series links.
- Adjust the text size and save your place in the same browser on the same device.

## A few places to start

- [The Fox in the Dandelion Sea](https://genshinbooks.allsbai.com/books/mondstadt/the-fox-in-the-dandelion-sea) — a hunter, a fox, and a wish to understand.
- [Rex Incognito](https://genshinbooks.allsbai.com/books/liyue/rex-incognito) — stories of Rex Lapis among mortals.
- [Toki Alley Tales](https://genshinbooks.allsbai.com/books/inazuma/toki-alley-tales) — tanuki, kitsune, and a boastful tengu.

## Sources

The primary text source is the English [HoYoWiki](https://wiki.hoyolab.com/pc/genshin/aggregate/book), the community-edited wiki hosted by HoYoLAB. Each reading identifies its source, including any retained earlier text variants. [About the collection and updates](https://genshinbooks.allsbai.com/about).
